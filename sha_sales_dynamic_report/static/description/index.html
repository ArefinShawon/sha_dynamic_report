Sales Report

Filter and review sales lines by date, salesperson, product, category and order status. Results are paginated for large databases and can be exported to Excel or PDF.

Features

  • Access-rule-aware sales-line reporting
  • Multi-company and multi-currency display
  • Server-side filtering and pagination
  • Excel and PDF exports
  • Clickable sale-order drill-down links

Requirements

Odoo 19.0 with Sales Management installed. Excel export requires the server's standard xlsxwriter dependency.
